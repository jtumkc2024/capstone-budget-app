Welcome to Smarter Save!

Your one-stop shop for budgeting!

What is Smarter Save?

Smarter Save is a project created by a group of College Students for a capstone class. It is a budgeting app, designed to help people who may not be familiar with budgeting. We use your card to connect you with your bank account, and from there we perform a series of searches to find your latest incoming transaction so we can get a rough estimate of how much you've made in the current pay period. From there, the app will give a series of suggestions based on the priority of your current saving projects; but it's up to you to set aside the money that's suggested. Just be sure to tell the app you've done so using the 'Pledge' button so we can get the most accurate measure of your current projects! Should you decide not to pledge the amount the app advises, no worries! None of your funds can be directly accessed or manipulated by the app. We exist purely as a tool to advise you on how you should save! There will be a guide on how to use the app further down, so if you're struggling with anything, please check it out!

I'm having trouble figuring out the app

We'll be glad to help! Smarter Save is easy to use and is dedicated to helping you learn how to budget! We'll go ahead and break down the basics.
Saving Projects
A saving project refers to a 'goal' you wish to accomplish. Projects can be as simple as a cash goal and a title or as complex as you allow, and you're able to customize them to best fit your needs! You can create a new saving project by clicking on the 'start saving' button in the navbar, and you can edit older projects by clicking 'edit'! A saving project only really needs a goal and a title to function, but we have a few optional fields to help you out as you use our service. Below are a few things you might encounter in the project menu that can help you adjust your projects to best fit your needs.
Priority
Priority is what determines how much the computer will assign your saving project. Priority is measured on a scale of 1-5, with '1' being the highest priority, think things like your rent or utilities! By contrast '5' is the lowest priority, something you don't need immediately- think things like a new video game! By default, your priority for a project is set to '3' or medium priority, so if you're not getting a whole lot of suggestions from the computer you find helpful, try adjusting your project priorities by editing the saving project!
Renewable Projects
Have something that comes back around once every month? Then this is the tool for you! This option is made with things like rent and utilities in mind. When checked, the computer will disregard the 'year' and 'month' aspects of the deadline it is assigned, instead it will renew every month on the day it was originally set to expire. When it renews, it will reset the goal using the initial goal that was given to it. It's a good way to keep track of how close you are to making rent or utilities that month! We do also recommend that if the saving project is marked as renewable, that you give the project a higher priority! It's never good to be caught off guard when the rent is due!
Project Description
This field in particular will have the least bearing on your project and its function; it's just for your convenience! It's hard to remember what something is for, especially if you name it something vague like 'For Mike', expecting to remember what 'For Mike' means in three months when the project expires, so we added a description field to help you keep track of the details of what you're saving for. The description won't harm your project if you decide not to do it, but we recommend jotting down something simple and descriptive!

I'm not getting any recommendations on my projects

That doesn't sound good! Double check that you've got a card added--if you don't, we don't have any way of reading your recent transaction and giving out advice! If you've got a card added, and the project still won't update, double check that your card information is up to date from the edit menu!
